## Project Overview

AchieverOS Lab is a comprehensive medical/scientific research operating system with:
- **8 specializations** (Clinical, Discovery, Genomic, Performance, Operations, Biotech, MedTech, Regulatory)
- **5 main pages** (Command Center, Ideation & Research, Visualization Studio, Literature Review, Business & Mediation)
- **93+ Figma wireframes** organized into 11 locked sections
- **~200 planned tools** across inquiry tools, sources, WASM solvers, and methodology templates
- **L0–L6 compute architecture** with client-side WASM, gated AI models, and agentic swarm
- **Full regulatory compliance** (21 CFR Part 11, HIPAA, GxP)
