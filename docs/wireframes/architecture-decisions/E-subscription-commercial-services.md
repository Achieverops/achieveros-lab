# E · Subscription + Commercial Services Governance

> Figma Node: `47:2101` · Architecture Decisions

## Software ≠ Professional Services

Entitlements may expand operating scope and auditability; they never imply better biological truth, approval, clinical success or probability of success.

## Five Software Subscriptions

- Neutral lab-tool names: Bench, Workbench, Studio, Lab, Network (working names)
- Proposed price points: $0, $99, $499, $1,499, $3,000/month — **VALIDATION REQUIRED**
- May vary: storage, compute, seats, workflow limits, integrations, review depth, audit/export capabilities
- Must not vary: evidence standards, scientific truth, safety claims or review integrity

## Professional Services

- Commercial mediation / feasibility are separate engagements, not subscription upgrades
- User hypotheses: $15k–$45k mediation; $300k–$500k feasibility — **VALIDATION REQUIRED**
- Each engagement requires intake, authority, COI, confidentiality, scope, method, exclusions, deliverables, SOW, approvals and change orders

## Disclosure Contract

- Show currency, billing period, tax, included usage, overage method, data retention, locality, support, cancellation and export rights
- For services show responsible party, external work, referral/sponsorship, assumptions, dependencies and legal/regulatory limits
- Hide fee split and turnaround until contracted and approved

## Prohibited Claims

- No outcome guarantees
- No “zero hallucinations”
- No improved biological truth, regulatory approval, clinical success or PoS by tier
- No “McKinsey-grade” customer-facing certification or endorsement
- No professional service presented as an instant checkout storefront
