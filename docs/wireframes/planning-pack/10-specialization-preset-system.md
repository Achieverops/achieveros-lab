# 10 · Specialization Preset System

> Figma Node: `40:1606` · Planning Pack · 14 Sep 2026

Presets tune schemas, routing and guidance while preserving the same five-page information architecture and object contracts.

## Eight Presets

1. Drug discovery & translational medicine
2. Clinical research
3. Biotechnology & omics
4. Materials science
5. Chemistry & formulation
6. Engineering & advanced manufacturing
7. Energy & climate
8. Food, agriculture & environmental science

## Preset Changes

1. Resource/tool defaults and typed connectors
2. Partner geography, facility and accreditation filters
3. Evidence fields, ontologies and controlled vocabularies
4. Compute profiles and cost ceilings
5. Assistant prompt packs and review checklists
6. Export templates and specialty QA

## Shared Foundation

1. Objects, permissions, lineage, citations
2. Five page shells and contextual surface rules
3. Workflow/run/job status model
4. INNOVATION 10-step envelope
5. Honest state vocabulary
6. Audit, approvals, exports and deep links

## Multi-Specialty Behavior

1. Project may activate multiple presets
2. Show shared core once; merge specialty extensions
3. Conflict banner when schemas or policy disagree
4. Primary preset controls defaults, never deletes data
5. Cross-specialty assistant labels lens used
6. Routing explains why each capability was selected
