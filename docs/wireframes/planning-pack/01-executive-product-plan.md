# 01 · Executive Product Plan

> Figma Node: `40:1242` · Planning Pack · 14 Sep 2026

## North Star

One continuous, evidence-grounded research operating system: inquiry becomes a traceable decision without losing context, provenance or human control.

## Five-Page Boundary

1. **Command Center** — resume work + exceptions
2. **Ideation & Research** — hypotheses and evidence
3. **Visualization Studio** — full-space analysis
4. **Literature Review** — stage-based evidence workflow
5. **Business & Mediation** — governed decision dossier
6. **Guided Journey**: excluded; one top-bar trigger only

## Design Principles

1. One dominant work surface per page
2. Canonical objects over page-local copies
3. Source-grounded answers; citation-gated promotion
4. Contextual extensions, immutable history
5. Honest readiness and visible provenance
6. Full-space viewers; no four-column middle

## Proposal Disposition

1. **ACCEPT**: 8 presets, INNOVATION, L0–L6, governed routing
2. **ACCEPT**: overlays, drawers, compare and source tray
3. **REJECT**: KPI mosaics, mini viewers, page-like ledgers
4. **DEFER**: cinematic Guided Journey and WebGPU
5. **VERIFY**: Gemini/NotebookLM, repository and LLM lanes
6. Never label Connected or Live without tests

## Priorities + Shippable

1. **P0**: truth, object model, shell, provenance, core states
2. **P1**: page workflows, presets, assistant, exports
3. **P2**: advanced swarm, partner routing, optimization
4. **Shippable** = five pages, deep links, access/audit, state recovery, E2E/security/perf tests
5. Approval required before frame edits or Cursor instructions
